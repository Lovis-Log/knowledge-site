## 英文原文

来源：[The Guardian view on global inequality: extreme wealth threatens democracy](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/aug/18/the-guardian-view-on-global-inequality-extreme-wealth-threatens-democracy)；The Guardian（社论），2026 年 8 月 19 日。

[1] In November, [Californians](https://theconversation.com/why-a-vote-on-taxing-californias-billionaires-is-being-watched-around-the-world-289302) will vote on a one-off 5% levy on residents worth more than $1bn. The union-backed plan could raise $100bn, more than enough to replace federal healthcare funding stripped away by [Donald Trump's One Big Beautiful Bill Act](https://www.ft.com/content/d66976cd-acdf-46d3-bb34-e2419873ecdb?syn-25a6b1a6=1). About 200 very rich people would pay. But the referendum is becoming a test of whether extreme wealth can buy the political power to [defeat](https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-08-14/californians-split-on-proposed-tax-on-billionaires-sour-on-voter-id-requirement-poll-shows) a popular redistributive policy. Google co-founder Sergey Brin has already spent $100m backing opposition to the tax – a fraction of the $13bn bill he could face. Other [billionaires](https://www.ft.com/content/10aba768-20fc-46eb-8503-4658181bffc5) are joining him. Whether voters can impose costs on the super-rich once their fortunes are big enough to shape the political process is now an open question. That alone should trouble any democracy.

[2] It's a long way from Mr Brin's west coast lair of [Mountain View](https://visit.withgoogle.com/) to Andy Burnham's Makerfield constituency. But the prime minister should heed the call from prominent progressive academics – led by the Nobel prize winner [Joseph Stiglitz](https://www.theguardian.com/inequality/2026/aug/18/economists-andy-burnham-uk-un-initiative-global-international-panel-inequality) – urging Britain to join a new global push to tackle inequality. Prof Stiglitz wants help creating a new UN International Panel on Inequality (IPI). This stems from the G20's [first-ever inequality report](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202511/g20-global-inequality-report-full-and-summary.pdf), which warned that the yawning gap between rich and poor was a human-made crisis corroding democracy and social cohesion while blocking efforts to tackle the climate emergency.

[3] The academics argue that Mr Burnham's Makerfield test – which aims to be fairer to places neglected by Westminster – should apply abroad as well as at home. By connecting the government's domestic programme to its foreign policy, a distinct Burnhamism could take shape. Between 2000 and 2024, the richest 1% captured 41% of new global wealth; the poorest half received 1%. Britain mirrors this divide. The economist [Gabriel Zucman](https://www.bigissue.com/opinion/gabriel-zucman-why-britain-must-tax-billionaire-wealth/) says the UK's richest 0.001% – about 200 families – held wealth worth 5% of GDP in 1989. Today, those families command an astonishing 25% of the country's GDP.

[4] When Mr Burnham [travels to the UN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_international_prime_ministerial_trips_made_by_Andy_Burnham) next month, he should back the IPI and make tackling global inequality a priority of Britain's 2027 G20 presidency. Prof Stiglitz argues that extreme inequality is a policy choice, turbocharged by financial deregulation, weaker labour protections and privatisation. Highly unequal countries are [seven times](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2025/nov/30/the-guardian-view-on-the-inequality-emergency-why-a-nobel-prize-winners-warning-must-be-heeded) more likely to suffer democratic backsliding or authoritarian drift. Taxing the [ultra-wealthy](https://peri.umass.edu/publication/g20-global-inequality-report/) should be part of Britain's response to a worldwide emergency.

[5] After Labour's aid cuts, Mr Burnham also needs a credible way of saying Britain is back in global development. The IPI gives him an inexpensive initiative around which to build reform of [international taxation](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/aug/02/the-guardian-view-on-global-corporate-tax-a-500bn-prize-that-states-must-seize), debt relief and illicit financial flows. None of this suggests that the UK wants an unproductive relationship with Washington. The White House is threatening the UK over its plans for a digital services tax, with the US [warning](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/politics/trump-tariff-uk-global-digital-facebook-google-b3034696.html) that Mr Trump's threat of retaliatory 100% tariffs is real. Policy disagreement need not poison the relationship.

[6] Mr Trump has denounced Zohran Mamdani's New York luxury second-home tax as a "[dangerous political experiment](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-floats-possible-legal-challenge-new-york-tax-luxury-second-homes-2026-08-11/)" and threatened to block it. Yet despite clashing, the leftwing mayor and the rightwing president [continue](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/white-house/4531421/mamdani-trump-relationship-in-touch-despite-disagreements/) to talk, [cooperating](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/white-house/4531421/mamdani-trump-relationship-in-touch-despite-disagreements/) on [housing](https://www.fox5ny.com/news/mamdani-trump-sunnyside-yard-development) and [immigration](https://apnews.com/article/columbia-university-student-dhs-ice-f76e678d014680390322703d46eb3b9c) cases. Their warm White House [meeting](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/feb/27/mamdani-trump-white-house-meeting) in February suggests that Mr Trump may respect politicians who openly disagree with him more than those who bend over backwards not to offend him. Perhaps Mr Burnham should take heart: being true to himself may work.

## 长难句拆解

### 主语从句 + once 条件

**原句**：[1] Whether voters can impose costs on the super-rich once their fortunes are big enough to shape the political process is now an open question.

**主干**：主语是 `Whether voters can impose costs on the super-rich ...`，谓语 `is now an open question`。

**层次与易错点**：主语从句内部先有 `once their fortunes are big enough to shape the political process` 这个条件从句，再有 `to shape` 作 enough 的补足语。易错点：once 在此是「一旦」，不是「一次」；主语从句很长，读到 poll 时容易以为 voters 就是谓语的主语，实际上整个 whether 从句才是主语。

**逻辑关系**：条件 + 判断——一旦财富大到能左右政治，选民还能否对其课以成本，尚无定论。

**参考译文**：一旦财富大到足以左右政治进程，选民还能否让超级富豪承担代价，如今已是一个没有答案的问题。

**写作迁移**：*Whether small towns can keep their young people once transport links improve is still unclear.* 一旦交通改善，小城镇能否留住年轻人仍不清楚。

### 插入语与分词后置修饰

**原句**：[2] But the prime minister should heed the call from prominent progressive academics – led by the Nobel prize winner Joseph Stiglitz – urging Britain to join a new global push to tackle inequality.

**主干**：主句 `the prime minister should heed the call`；`from prominent progressive academics` 说明谁发出的呼吁。

**层次与易错点**：两个破折号之间的 `led by ...` 修饰的是 academics，表示由谁牵头；后半 `urging Britain to join ...` 是现在分词短语，同样修饰 academics，说明呼吁的内容。易错点：若把 urging 看成修饰 call 或 prime minister，句意会跑偏；破折号内的 Nobel prize winner 是同位语，不是另一个人。

**逻辑关系**：补充说明——谁在呼吁、呼吁什么。

**参考译文**：但首相应当听取一批知名进步派学者的呼吁——这批学者由诺贝尔奖得主约瑟夫·斯蒂格利茨领衔——他们敦促英国加入一场应对不平等的新全球行动。

**写作迁移**：*The report, led by a former central banker, urges governments to publish debt data.* 这份由前央行官员牵头的报告，敦促各国政府公开债务数据。

### 非限定从句中的双重动作

**原句**：[2] This stems from the G20's first-ever inequality report, which warned that the yawning gap between rich and poor was a human-made crisis corroding democracy and social cohesion while blocking efforts to tackle the climate emergency.

**主干**：主句 `This stems from the ... report`；which 引导非限定性关系从句，其内部主句是 `the yawning gap ... was a human-made crisis`。

**层次与易错点**：`corroding democracy and social cohesion` 是现在分词修饰 crisis，`while blocking efforts ...` 与 corroding 并列，同为 crisis 造成的影响。易错点：while 在此表「同时」，不是让步；若把 blocking 当作 warned 的宾语，会漏掉「腐蚀」与「阻碍」这两个并列后果。

**逻辑关系**：原因（报告内容）+ 并列后果。

**参考译文**：这源于 G20 首份不平等报告，该报告警告说，贫富之间悬殊的差距是人为造成的危机，它腐蚀民主与社会凝聚力，同时阻碍应对气候紧急状态的努力。

**写作迁移**：*The study warns that long commutes are a hidden cost draining incomes and eroding family time.* 该研究警告说，长时间通勤是一项隐性成本，既消耗收入，又侵蚀家庭时间。

### 过去分词作后置定语

**原句**：[4] Prof Stiglitz argues that extreme inequality is a policy choice, turbocharged by financial deregulation, weaker labour protections and privatisation.

**主干**：`Prof Stiglitz argues that ...`；宾语从句主句是 `extreme inequality is a policy choice`。

**层次与易错点**：`turbocharged by ...` 是过去分词短语，修饰 choice，说明这一「选择」被哪些政策放大；三个并列名词共用 by。易错点：turbocharged 原意是「加装涡轮增压」，此处引申为「被极大加速」，不能直译为「被涡轮增压」；把三个并列项看成选择的内容而非原因，也会读错。

**逻辑关系**：因果——不平等并非自然结果，而是政策选择被某些政策放大的产物。

**参考译文**：斯蒂格利茨教授认为，极端不平等是一种政策选择，而金融去监管、劳动保护削弱和私有化使其进一步加剧。

**写作迁移**：*Youth unemployment is a policy failure, worsened by cuts to training schemes.* 青年失业是政策失败，而培训计划的削减使其雪上加霜。

## 术语与行业黑话

- **levy**｜本文含义：征收的税款｜使用背景：常指一次性或专项税费，与常规所得税区分｜近词辨析：levy 可作名词与动词，tax 更通用；levy on 表示对某物征税｜可否迁移到雅思：可（税收类话题）。
- **redistributive policy**｜本文含义：再分配政策，通过税收与福利改变收入分配｜使用背景：政治经济学常用，争论集中在效率与公平｜近词辨析：与 welfare policy 有重叠，但再分配强调资金来源与流向｜可否迁移到雅思：可。
- **IPI（International Panel on Inequality）**｜本文含义：拟设的联合国不平等专门小组｜使用背景：仿照气候领域 IPCC 的模式，由学者提供共识性报告｜近词辨析：IPI 是专家小组，不是有执法权的机构｜可否迁移到雅思：可（作具体例证）。
- **democratic backsliding**｜本文含义：民主制度逐步退化｜使用背景：政治学概念，指选举仍在但制衡被削弱的渐进过程｜近词辨析：与 coup（政变）不同，backsliding 是渐变而非突变｜可否迁移到雅思：可。
- **illicit financial flows**｜本文含义：非法资金流动，如逃税、洗钱｜使用背景：发展经济学议题，常与税基侵蚀并列讨论｜近词辨析：不等于 capital flight（资本外逃），后者未必违法｜可否迁移到雅思：可（需解释）。
- **GDP**｜本文含义：国内生产总值，衡量一国经济规模｜使用背景：此处用来表示某群体财富相当于全国经济产出的比重｜近词辨析：GDP 是流量，wealth 是存量，二者相除只是一种比例表达｜可否迁移到雅思：可。

## 背景补充

- **事实**：加州拟议的税针对净资产超过 10 亿美元的居民，预计约 200 人缴纳，可筹集约 1000 亿美元（[1]）。社论用这些数字说明「少数人承担、多数人受益」的设计。
- **事实**：G20 首次发布不平等报告，学者据此推动设立联合国不平等专门小组（[2]）。这是文中一切政策建议的来源。
- **事实**：2000 至 2024 年，全球新增财富的 41% 流向最富的 1%，最穷的一半只得到 1%（[3]）。这是社论「不平等在扩大」的核心证据。
- **观点（社论）**：社论认为与美国总统公开分歧并不必然损害双边关系，并以纽约市长与特朗普的互动为例（[6]）。这是社论的推论，不是已被证明的规律。

## 雅思迁移

**写作**

题目：*Some people think governments should tax the very rich more heavily to reduce inequality. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*

提纲：
- 立场：基本同意，但强调税制设计比税率高低更关键。
- 主体一：财富集中会侵蚀机会平等与政治公平，适度再分配有正当性。
- 主体二：若缺乏国际协调，资本与高技能劳动力可能外流，效果打折。
- 结论：应以累进税配合国际合作为主，而非单次象征性征税。

示范段（82 词）：

> Taxing the very rich more heavily is defensible when wealth concentration begins to distort opportunity. When a small group can fund campaigns or relocate at will, ordinary voters lose influence over decisions that shape their lives. That said, the design matters as much as the rate. A poorly coordinated tax may simply push capital and skilled workers elsewhere, leaving the revenue target unmet. Progressive taxation therefore works best when it is paired with international cooperation rather than introduced as a one-off gesture.

**阅读**

1. 加州这项税为什么被社论称为对民主的检验？
   定位：[1]
   依据："whether extreme wealth can buy the political power to defeat a popular redistributive policy"
   答案：因为它检验的是：当财富大到能影响政治进程时，选民是否仍能对超级富豪征税。

2. 社论引用 G20 报告的哪些结论？
   定位：[2]
   依据："a human-made crisis corroding democracy and social cohesion"
   答案：报告认为贫富差距是人为危机，会腐蚀民主与社会凝聚力，并阻碍应对气候变化的努力。

3. 英国的不平等数据说明了什么？
   定位：[3]
   依据："those families command an astonishing 25% of the country's GDP"
   答案：最富的约 200 个家庭财富占 GDP 比重从 1989 年的 5% 升到如今的 25%，说明财富集中显著加剧。

4. 作者为什么提到英美之间的税收争端？
   定位：[5]
   依据："Policy disagreement need not poison the relationship."
   答案：为了说明英国在数字服务税等问题上与美国存在分歧，但这不等于要放弃国际合作。

5. 结尾用纽约市长的例子想说明什么？
   定位：[6]
   依据："being true to himself may work"
   答案：说明公开坚持立场未必损害合作，反而可能赢得尊重，借此鼓励伯纳姆坚持自己的主张。

## 口语延伸

**Part 2**

> **Describe a social issue that you think is important.**
> You should say:
> - what the issue is
> - how you learned about it
> - who it affects most
> and explain why you think it deserves attention.

2 分钟提纲（58 词）：`I would talk about the wealth gap. I first noticed it through a news report on a proposed tax for billionaires. It affects young families most, because housing and education costs rise faster than wages. It deserves attention because unequal societies tend to become less stable and less open to newcomers.`

**Part 3**

1. Should governments intervene when wealth becomes highly concentrated?（出处：[1]）
   思路：先承认市场效率，再讲政治公平的外部性。
   *Some intervention is justified, because extreme concentration can **distort political influence**, not just incomes. The key is to target the mechanism, such as campaign funding, rather than punishing success itself.*

2. Why do some wealthy people oppose higher taxes?（出处：[1]）
   思路：从自身成本、对政策效果的怀疑、对流动性的担忧三点答。
   *Partly it is self-interest, since the sums involved are large. Some also doubt that governments **spend tax revenue well**, and others worry that capital will simply move to countries with lighter rules.*

3. How can international cooperation make taxation more effective?（出处：[4]–[5]）
   思路：讲税基侵蚀、信息交换与共同最低标准。
   *If countries share information and agree on **minimum standards**, companies have fewer places to hide profits. Without coordination, a single country that raises taxes alone risks losing investment to its neighbours.*
