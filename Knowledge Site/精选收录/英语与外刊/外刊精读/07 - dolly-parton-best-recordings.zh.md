## 英文原文

来源：[From Jolene to Islands in the Stream: Dolly Parton's 10 best recordings](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/aug/26/dolly-parton-best-songs-jolene)；The Guardian，Rob LeDonne，2026 年 8 月 26 日。

[1] The [death of Dolly Parton today at the age of 80](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/aug/25/dolly-parton-country-singer-dead) has the world mourning one of America's most beloved entertainers: a generation-spanning performer and country music icon who took as much pride in her music as she did her sparkling appearance. Parton's impressive musical output across a 60-year career and a whopping 50 albums was built on a foundation of her undeniable knack for songwriting: ranging from heartfelt lyrics to catchy hooks. Here are just 10 of Parton's greatest contributions to the American musical lexicon.

### Jolene (1973)

[2] It's a country classic that straddles the line between tough and vulnerable, and according to an oft-told story by Parton, inspired by true events. Based on a real person Parton felt threatened by that her late husband Carl Dean knew, the music legend turned her anxiety into some of the sharpest lines of her career which hum along on an unforgettable melody. As legend has it, the real person was a red-headed clerk at a local bank. Meanwhile, the actual name [came from a young fan](https://www.tiktok.com/@dollyparton/video/7209049975018523950) she signed an autograph for; the chorus came from Parton repeating Jolene over and over so she wouldn't later forget it. Landing on multiple lists that rank the greatest songs of all time, Jolene also holds the distinction of being nominated for a Grammy for best female country vocal performance twice: once for the original track in 1975, and then again for a live recording a year later.

### I Will Always Love You (1974)

[3] Demonstrating Parton's undeniable knack for songwriting, one of the most amazing stories in the annals of music history is that Parton wrote Jolene and her tour de force romantic anthem I Will Always Love You within a stunningly short span of each other. (The previous legend stated that she wrote them on the same day, which Parton [humbly disputed](https://www.kentucky.com/news/nation-world/national/article259049603.html) in 2022). Funnily enough, Parton didn't write this one in tribute to her husband: instead, it was an ode to mentor Porter Wagoner, with Parton penning the track in light of going solo after closely collaborating with the fellow country star. "If I should stay, I would only be in your way," she memorably croons as the song opens. Sure, Whitney Houston would later make the song her own, but Parton's version is just as legendary.

### 9 to 5 (1980)

[4] Get that steaming cup of ambition! It's a working-class anthem for the ages, and Parton makes the office grind sound kind of fun. Recorded for the 1980 comedy 9 to 5 which starred Parton alongside Lily Tomlin and Jane Fonda as hard workers with an ax to, well, grind, both the blockbuster movie and song launched Parton firmly into the mainstream upon its release. The bouncy tune kicks off with that pulsating piano, almost mimicking a machine, before busting into its instantly memorable chorus and it was all inspired by her nails. On set, Parton would scrape her acrylics together, with the rhythm and beat born out of a unique movement only Parton could concoct. The 9 to 5 track itself became Parton's first Hot 100 No 1 hit, and it would later inspire a 2009 musical.

### Coat of Many Colors (1971)

[5] Any fan of Parton knows about her hardscrabble youth; growing up in poverty in the mountains of rural Tennessee before she evolved into the dazzling legend we know her as today. Parton never forgot those humble beginnings and would share her inspirational story in a series of projects throughout her life. That includes her signature song Coat of Many Colors which was inspired by a chilly Tennessee fall back in the day. "I recall a box of rags that someone gave us, and how my momma put the rags to use," she delicately sings in the tender ode ripped from her childhood. "Momma sewed the rags together, sewin' every piece with love, she made my coat of many colors, I was so proud of." OK, where are the tissues?

### Islands in the Stream (1983)

[6] Take one listen to Islands in the Stream and it will be impossible to get the earworm out of your head. Holding the distinction as Parton's second-ever Hot 100 No 1 hit, on Islands in the Stream she's paired perfectly with the country crooner Kenny Rogers on the sweet and smooth track which bubbles along much like its eponymous waterway. The song is also an outlier in Parton's discography, as it's one of a handful of trademark songs she *didn't* write. (The Bee Gees, Barry, Maurice and Robin Gibb, are credited on this one.) Nevertheless, the combination of all five unique personalities turned the bouncy track into one of the best duets of all time and a bona fide karaoke staple.

### Here You Come Again (1977)

[7] In addition to Islands in the Stream, Here You Come Again is another rare track Parton didn't pen. Here You Come Again, her first gold record, was penned by the Brill Building legends Barry Mann and Cynthia Weil, with the buoyant ditty sounding like such a surefire hit to Parton, she was reluctant to record it. "She said a monkey could sing this song and have a hit with it," her manager, Sandy Gallin, [said](https://americansongwriter.com/a-songwriting-accident-turned-into-one-of-dolly-partons-biggest-hits-in-1977/) in 2016. "And she was very nervous that it would turn off the country market, which she was very loyal to. There was no way she wanted to ever let anything insinuate that she may be turning her back on the country audience." Parton need not have worried: country fans embraced the tune so much that it lodged five weeks at No 1 on the country charts, later winning a Grammy.

### Two Doors Down (1978)

[8] Deftly mixing Parton's country twang with the panache of pop, further evidence of Parton's crossover success between the two genres can be heard on Two Doors Down, which continues Jolene's themes of loneliness. Lyrically, she portrayed an isolated woman whose lover is having a brouhaha right down the hall. Oddly enough, [as Parton told Kelly Clarkson in 2022](https://www.tiktok.com/@partonnews/video/7173427894335802670), it was inspired by a diet Parton was on at the time. She and her band were staying at a Howard Johnson's Motor Lodge. But while her band was feasting on fried clams and having a good ol' time, Parton sulking in her room, sticking to only consuming liquid protein. "I could hear them all laughing and talking, and I just started singing: two doors down, they're laughing and drinking and having a party," she recalled.

### Why'd You Come in Here Lookin' Like That? (1989)

[9] Marking Parton's 22nd No 1 on the country charts upon its late-80s release, Why'd You Come in Here Lookin' Like That? is classic Parton, with the energetic track dripping with the attitude and sass she will forever be known for. The first to crack a joke about her appearance (her favorite saying: "It costs a lot of money to look this cheap"), Parton was proudly vain and widely extravagant in her looks, from her fashion, nails and hair. Naturally, she would strike a chord with a song where she flips the narrative, focusing on an old flame who's flaunting "cowboy boots and your painted-on jeans, all decked out like a cowgirl's dream". The only thing sharper than Parton's looks was her tongue.

### Rockstar (2023)

[10] Most artists slow down, peter out, sell out and offer diminished returns as the decades trudge on. Parton, meanwhile, was roaring on all cylinders into her 70s, with the superstar continually releasing an array of unique new projects. Her last full album was 2023's Rockstar, a cover album which saw Parton duet with everyone from goddaughter Miley Cyrus to Paul McCartney and Elton John on a fun and silly series of rock'n'roll covers. Parton, however, had to put her own mark on the project, writing a title track that opens up the melodic bacchanal. Starting with a thrashing guitar courtesy of Richie Sambora, Rockstar symbolizes her generation-spanning relevance and a passionate devotion to pure creativity.

### If You Hadn't Been There (2025)

[11] Perhaps it's fitting that one of the very last singles Parton released during her eight decades was last spring's If You Hadn't Been There, a tearjerker love song to her beloved husband who died the week before. "If you hadn't been there, where would I be?" Parton lusciously asks in her unmistakable whisper-tone as a piano tickles in the background. The late-career song is a perfect example of Parton's access to unbridled emotion: as much as she was unafraid to let her hair down, she was also fearlessly vulnerable and emotive. As an artist, that yin and yang is a golden encapsulation of what made her so special. And now, legions of music fans are asking the same question about Dolly herself: if you hadn't been there, where would we be?

> 编者更正（2026 年 8 月 26 日）：较早版本称，在《Why'd You Come in Here Looking Like That?》中帕顿是在批评另一位女性的外貌，实际上她指的是前男友。

## 文章脉络

歌单式特写：以去世消息开篇，随后按时间顺序挑出十首作品，每首一段，兼顾创作背景与演唱细节。这种「十篇小传」结构本身就是一次论证——用作品序列说明她的跨代影响力。

阅读提醒：这是纪念性特写，作者对每首歌的评价都带明显褒扬，不是客观评分。选曲标准也未说明，属编辑趣味；「十大」不等于销量或评论界共识。文末的编者更正还提示了一点：连这种看似简单的作品介绍也可能出错，读引语与事实陈述时要留意媒体后来是否作了修改。读完后不妨自问：这十首里，哪一首最能代表她的哪一面？

## 长难句拆解

### 同位语追加与冒号列举

**原句**：[1] Parton's impressive musical output across a 60-year career and a whopping 50 albums was built on a foundation of her undeniable knack for songwriting: ranging from heartfelt lyrics to catchy hooks.

**主干**：主句 `Parton's impressive musical output ... was built on a foundation of her undeniable knack for songwriting`。

**层次与易错点**：主语后面跟了两个并列的修饰成分——`across a 60-year career` 与 `and a whopping 50 albums`，注意后者不是与 output 并列的主语；冒号后 `ranging from ... to ...` 补充说明 knack 的具体表现。易错点：把 and a whopping 50 albums 当成第二个主语会找不到谓语；a whopping 是「高达、数量惊人」的口语强调，不是「巨大的」修饰专辑本身。

**逻辑关系**：总述 + 具体化（冒号后给出例证）。

**参考译文**：帕顿在六十年生涯中、五十张专辑里留下的音乐成果，建立在不可否认的创作天赋之上：从真挚的歌词到抓耳的旋律。

**写作迁移**：*The library's collection, built across four decades and thousands of donations, rests on one principle: books should be shared.* 这座图书馆的藏书历经四十年、上千次捐赠累积而成，依据的是一项原则：书应当被分享。

### 多重后置修饰

**原句**：[2] Based on a real person Parton felt threatened by that her late husband Carl Dean knew, the music legend turned her anxiety into some of the sharpest lines of her career which hum along on an unforgettable melody.

**主干**：主句 `the music legend turned her anxiety into some of the sharpest lines of her career`；句首是过去分词短语作状语。

**层次与易错点**：`Based on a real person` 是状语开头，随后 `Parton felt threatened by` 是省略了关系词的定语从句修饰 person，`that her late husband ... knew` 又是另一个定语从句，同样修饰 person；句末 `which hum along ...` 修饰 lines。易错点：连续两个定语从句都指向 person，容易把第二个理解成修饰 husband；`hum along` 是说歌词朗朗上口，不是「哼着唱」。另一个常见误读是把 `felt threatened by` 当作主句谓语——真正的谓语是后面的 turned，前面整段都只是状语。

**逻辑关系**：来源 + 转化（把焦虑变成歌词）。

**参考译文**：这首歌取材于一位让帕顿感到威胁的真实人物——她已故的丈夫卡尔·迪恩认识这个人——她把这份焦虑化成了自己生涯中最锋利的几句歌词，而这几句又叮咚流淌在一段难忘的旋律里。

**写作迁移**：*Inspired by a letter her grandfather never opened, the novelist turned a family silence into the sharpest chapter of her book.* 受一封祖父从未拆开的信启发，这位小说家把一段家族的沉默写成了全书最锋利的一章。

### 冒号解释与 so...that 结果

**原句**：[7] Parton need not have worried: country fans embraced the tune so much that it lodged five weeks at No 1 on the country charts, later winning a Grammy.

**主干**：冒号前是结论 `Parton need not have worried`，冒号后用事实说明为什么不必担心。

**层次与易错点**：`need not have worried` 表示「本不必担心」（对过去的判断），与 need not worry 不同；`so much that` 引出结果；`later winning` 是分词作补充结果。易错点：把 need not have worried 理解为「不必担心（将来）」会丢掉「事后证明」的语气；lodged 在此是「停留在（某个名次）」，不是「寄宿」。另外，冒号后的两个分句各有主语——country fans 与 it，不要读成一句连续的动作。

**逻辑关系**：结论 + 证据。

**参考译文**：帕顿本不必担心：乡村乐迷太喜欢这首歌了，它在乡村音乐榜冠军位置停留了五周，后来还赢得格莱美。

**写作迁移**：*The team need not have worried: supporters filled the stadium so quickly that tickets sold out within an hour.* 球队本不必担心：球迷涌入球场如此之快，门票一小时内售罄。

### as much as 的双重对照

**原句**：[11] The late-career song is a perfect example of Parton's access to unbridled emotion: as much as she was unafraid to let her hair down, she was also fearlessly vulnerable and emotive.

**主干**：主句 `The late-career song is a perfect example of ...`；冒号后用 `as much as ... , she was also ...` 作解释。

**层次与易错点**：as much as 在此不是「和……一样多」，而是「尽管、虽然」，等于 although；前后两项是对照：敢于放松 vs 敢于脆弱。易错点：把 as much as 当比较结构会读成「她放松的程度和她脆弱的程度一样」，句意虽不荒谬却丢掉让步关系；unbridled、fearlessly 都是程度加强词。

**逻辑关系**：让步 + 并列补充。

**参考译文**：这首晚期作品完美体现了帕顿对无拘无束情感的掌控：尽管她敢于彻底放松，她也同样敢于毫不设防地脆弱与动情。

**写作迁移**：*As much as the design is playful, it is also rigorously tested.* 尽管这个设计很俏皮，它同样经过了严格测试。

## 术语与行业黑话

- **crossover**｜本文含义：跨界，指艺人或作品从一种音乐类型进入更广的市场｜使用背景：乡村歌手进入流行榜常被称为 crossover success｜近词辨析：crossover 指跨类型传播，remix 指对同一首歌重新编曲｜可否迁移到雅思：可（文化类话题）。
- **earworm**｜本文含义：挥之不去的旋律｜使用背景：音乐心理学的通俗说法，带调侃意味｜近词辨析：与 catchy（形容歌好听易记）不同，earworm 强调的是「赶不走」｜可否迁移到雅思：可（口语描述音乐）。
- **Hot 100 / country charts**｜本文含义：美国公告牌的两个榜单，前者是全类型单曲榜，后者是乡村类型榜｜使用背景：报道用两个榜单区分「流行成功」与「类型内成功」｜近词辨析：两者不可互换，No 1 的含金量不同｜可否迁移到雅思：不可，需解释背景。
- **gold record**｜本文含义：销量达到认证标准的唱片｜使用背景：各国认证门槛不同，美国由 RIAA 认定｜近词辨析：gold 是认证等级，不是奖项；与 Grammy（评奖）不同｜可否迁移到雅思：可（音乐产业话题）。
- **eponymous**｜本文含义：以（歌名）命名的｜使用背景：文中指这首歌与它所唱的那条河同名｜近词辨析：eponymous 指「以某人/物命名」，不是「同名同姓」的 namesake｜可否迁移到雅思：可（书面表达）。
- **country twang**｜本文含义：乡村音乐特有的鼻音唱腔｜使用背景：常被当作乡村歌手的辨识标志，也带一点地域刻板印象｜近词辨析：twang 指音色与口音，drawl 指拖长音节的说话方式｜可否迁移到雅思：可（描述音乐与口音）。
- **the Brill Building**｜本文含义：纽约一栋办公楼，1960 年代集中了大量职业词曲作者｜使用背景：指代「流水线式专业创作」的模式，与创作歌手相对｜近词辨析：它是一处地点与一种创作传统，不是唱片公司｜可否迁移到雅思：不可，需解释。

## 背景补充

- **事实**：帕顿于 2026 年 8 月 25 日去世，享年 80 岁（[1]）。这篇特写发表于次日，属纪念性报道。
- **事实**：她的生涯跨越六十年、五十张专辑（[1]），这次入选的十首从 1971 年的《Coat of Many Colors》到 2025 年的《If You Hadn't Been There》。
- **事实**：《I Will Always Love You》写给她单飞前的合作者、导师 Porter Wagoner（[3]）；1992 年惠特妮·休斯顿的翻唱使其再度流行。
- **观点（作者）**：作者认为她的特别之处在于「敢于放松」与「敢于脆弱」并存（[11]）。这是评论判断，不是可验证的事实。
- **事实**：《Jolene》曾两度获得格莱美最佳乡村女歌手演唱提名——一次凭 1975 年的原版，一次凭次年的现场版（[2]）。同一首歌两度入围同一奖项，这也是它在报道中被放在首位的原因之一。

## 雅思迁移

**写作**

题目：*Some people think popular music today lacks the quality of music from earlier decades. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*

提纲：
- 立场：不完全同意——技术改变了传播与聆听方式，但创作水准未必下降。
- 主体一：旧时代作品经过时间筛选，我们记住的是最好的部分，容易高估过去。
- 主体二：今天的音乐类型更细分、制作门槛更低，优秀作品依然存在，只是更难被统一评价。
- 结论：与其比较代际优劣，不如看音乐是否仍能提供情感与共同记忆。

示范段（81 词）：

> Comparing today's music with the past is harder than it looks. What survives from earlier decades is a small selection of the best work, while we hear everything produced now, including the forgettable parts. Streaming has also fragmented audiences, so no single song shapes a generation in the way it once did. That does not mean quality has fallen. It means success is measured differently, and a fair judgement should compare like with like.

**阅读**

1. 为什么说《Jolene》介于强硬与脆弱之间？
   定位：[2]
   依据："straddles the line between tough and vulnerable"
   答案：它取材于帕顿真实的焦虑——丈夫认识的一位女性令她感到威胁——歌词锋利，却建立在不安全感之上，因此两种气质并存。

2. 《I Will Always Love You》真正的写作对象是谁？
   定位：[3]
   依据："it was an ode to mentor Porter Wagoner"
   答案：不是丈夫，而是她的导师、乡村歌手波特·瓦格纳，写于她决定单飞之际。

3. 帕顿为什么一度不愿录《Here You Come Again》？
   定位：[7]
   依据："she was very nervous that it would turn off the country market"
   答案：她觉得这首歌太像稳中的流行金曲，担心会让乡村听众以为她要背弃乡村音乐。

4. 《Two Doors Down》的灵感来自什么？
   定位：[8]
   依据："it was inspired by a diet Parton was on at the time"
   答案：来自她减肥期间的真实处境——乐队在隔壁大快朵颐，她只能喝液体蛋白，于是写下这段「两门之隔」的歌词。

5. 作者用《If You Hadn't Been There》说明什么？
   定位：[11]
   依据："that yin and yang is a golden encapsulation of what made her so special"
   答案：说明她既敢放松也敢脆弱，两种气质并存正是她的独特之处；结尾把歌名反问到听众身上作收束。

## 口语延伸

**Part 2**

> **Describe a singer or musician you admire.**
> You should say:
> - who this person is
> - what kind of music they make
> - when you first heard their work
> and explain why you admire them.

2 分钟提纲（58 词）：`I would talk about Dolly Parton, a country singer who kept writing and performing into her seventies. I first heard her songs through a film soundtrack, without knowing who sang them. What I admire most is her honesty — she wrote about jealousy, poverty and grief in plain words and never pretended to be someone else.`

**Part 3**

1. Why do some musicians remain popular across generations?（出处：[10]）
   思路：讲作品的可迁移情感、持续产出与公众形象。
   *Their songs deal with feelings that do not date, so each generation **finds its own meaning** in them. Staying visible also helps, but without that emotional core it rarely lasts.*

2. Is it harder for artists today to build a long career?（出处：[10]）
   思路：区分曝光机会与注意力持续时间。
   *It may be easier to be noticed and harder to stay. Platforms reward constant output, yet **attention moves on quickly**, so building a body of work takes more deliberate effort.*

3. Should schools teach students about popular music?（出处：[2]）
   思路：从文化史与创作方法两方面答。
   *Yes, mainly because it is a record of how ordinary people felt in a given period. It is also a practical way to teach songwriting — **structure, repetition and imagery** can be studied in three minutes.*
