## 英文原文

来源：[US tech stocks see sharp falls as AI spending nears $1tn](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvwy72e0n72yo)；BBC，Rachel Clun，2026 年 10 月 30 日。

[1] Tech stocks tumbled for the second day in a row as concerns rise over the hundreds of billions of dollars firms are investing in artificial intelligence (AI).

[2] Shares in Intel plummeted by 28% on Friday, while Google owner Alphabet saw its stock price continue to fall another 1.4% after a 12% plunge on Thursday.

[3] Other tech stocks, including Reddit and Amazon also fell, while the Nasdaq index of the largest tech firms listed in the US, ended October with its worst monthly slide since March 2025.

[4] While tech firms are spending big on AI, their earnings are not always meeting analysts' expectations, spooking investors and creating fears of an AI bubble.

[5] Major tech companies have ramped up artificial intelligence spending, with Meta, Alphabet, Oracle, Amazon, and Microsoft committing to spending $800bn between them in 2026.

[6] Alphabet's stock market value fell sharply on Thursday, [taking more than $500bn (£374bn) off its market value](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm9qzdqpxy48o) after it revealed the AI summaries it now provides as the top answer to search queries were affecting its core search advertising revenue.

[7] At the same time, Alphabet expects to spend up to $205bn this year on investments, almost entirely related to AI.

[8] The bigger losses on Friday were elsewhere however, with Intel shedding more than $130bn in value.

[9] The chip maker said it planned to increase spending on AI despite weaker demand for computers, a major source of demand for its micro-processors.

[10] On Thursday, Intel's finance chief David Zinsner said the firm did not expect to bring in more cash than it spent this year and forecast lower-than-expected revenue for its fourth quarter.

[11] "Looking ahead, we see the PC slowdown continuing at least through the first half of next year," he said in the chip maker's third quarter earnings report.

[12] In the earnings call, he said the firm had significantly ramped up investment in equipment in the third quarter and expected that to be "even more significant" in the following three months.

### Amazon and Reddit down

[13] Amazon on Thursday revealed it had also spent more cash than it was making, and that spending had increased six-fold over the span of three months.

[14] The company has committed to spending $220bn this year, putting the money towards investments such as AI data centres, network equipment, and servers.

[15] Amazon shares recovered some overnight losses, but were still down by 1.4% at the end of Friday trading.

[16] Reddit stock plunged by 29%, after the social media platform revealed weaker-than-expected daily user figures and warned that Google's growing AI-driven search was hurting referral traffic.

[17] Stock in online game platform Roblox also fell, down by 18%, as its daily active user numbers dropped.

[18] Several major tech firms have reported earnings this week, including Microsoft, and analysts said there were questions about the payout from the huge investments in AI.

[19] Chris Low, from investment management firm FHN Financial, said the bulk of what was happening in the stock market was in reaction to some of the biggest tech players such as Amazon and Google.

[20] He told Reuters the companies were "showing some pretty significant growing pains as they aggressively pursue the AI build-out".

[21] "The thinking is that these signs suggest that the entire sector is going to slow down."

### 'Investors have turned ruthless'

[22] Many analysts have been debating the possibility of an AI bubble in the stock market.

[23] The worry is that some tech firms will not be able to justify their sky-high valuations because the cost of building AI data centres and developing the software will end up being greater than the money they can make from it.

[24] Kathleen Brookes, research director at trading firm XTB, said there have been many ups and downs in AI trade in recent months.

[25] "Investors have turned ruthless, punishing any signs of disappointment in [third quarter] earnings," she said.

[26] "The AI trade has had a good run in recent years, but now investors want payback, and companies like Alphabet and Intel are in the crosshairs."

[27] However, stock market values for many of these companies remain well above where they were a year ago.

[28] While the Nasdaq closed 2.8% lower, it is up 7.8% over the last year. Similarly Amazon is up nearly 8% over the past 12 months, while Alphabet is down 1.3%.

[29] Brookes noted that November is typically a strong month for stocks.

[30] "If we see AI stocks continue to struggle in the coming days, it could suggest the AI trade is past its peak, and investors will be looking for a new theme to drive markets," she said.

## 长难句拆解

### 让步状语从句叠加两个分词结果状语

**原句**：[4] While tech firms are spending big on AI, their earnings are not always meeting analysts' expectations, spooking investors and creating fears of an AI bubble.

**主干**：主句是 `their earnings are not always meeting analysts' expectations`，由 While 引导的让步从句限定；句末两个 -ing 短语作结果状语，逻辑主语是整个主句所述情况。

**层次与易错点**：第一层是 While 从句与主句的对照；第二层是 `not always` 构成的部分否定；第三层是两个并列分词短语 `spooking...` 与 `creating...`，说明这种落差造成的后果。易错点：把 While 读成时间状语会译成「当企业大举投入时，业绩没有达标」，丢掉让步关系，也读不出作者的立场——投入在涨，预期没跟上。

**逻辑关系**：让步 + 结果。

**参考译文**：尽管科技公司在 AI 上投入巨大，其业绩却并不总能达到分析师预期，这令投资者不安，也引发了对 AI 泡沫的担忧。

**写作迁移**：*While governments are investing heavily in public transport, the benefits may take years to emerge, leaving commuters sceptical.* 尽管政府在公共交通上投入巨大，效益可能要多年后才显现，这让通勤者心存疑虑。

### 并列谓语与否定词的管辖范围

**原句**：[10] Intel's finance chief David Zinsner said the firm did not expect to bring in more cash than it spent this year and forecast lower-than-expected revenue for its fourth quarter.

**主干**：`said` 后接一个宾语从句，从句内有两个并列谓语 `did not expect to...` 与 `forecast...`，共用主语 the firm。

**层次与易错点**：宾语从句第一层是 `did not expect` + 宾语 `to bring in more cash than it spent this year`，其中 `than it spent` 是比较从句；第二层是与 `did not expect` 并列的 `forecast lower-than-expected revenue for its fourth quarter`。易错点：`and forecast` 只与 `did not expect` 并列，不被否定词管辖；若把 forecast 读进否定范围，会译成「公司不预期下调收入预期」，意思正好相反。

**逻辑关系**：两个谓语由 and 并列，共同说明同一次表态，不是因果或转折。

**参考译文**：英特尔财务负责人戴维·津斯纳表示，公司预计今年的现金流入不会超过支出，并预测第四季度营收低于预期。

**写作迁移**：*Analysts said the firm did not expect demand to recover this year and forecast weaker sales for the next quarter.* 分析人士称，该公司预计今年需求不会回升，并预测下一季度销售走弱。

### 表语从句与比较结构的指代

**原句**：[23] The worry is that some tech firms will not be able to justify their sky-high valuations because the cost of building AI data centres and developing the software will end up being greater than the money they can make from it.

**主干**：主句是 `The worry is that...`，表语从句里套一个 because 引导的原因状语从句；原因从句的主语是 `the cost of building AI data centres and developing the software`。

**层次与易错点**：第一层 `be able to justify` 表示「证明……合理」；第二层原因从句里，`will end up being greater than` 把成本与收入作比较；第三层 `the money they can make from it` 中 they 指科技公司，it 指前文的 AI，不是紧邻的 software。易错点：把 it 误指 software 会读成「从软件中赚钱」，把范围缩小了一层；把 greater than 看成修饰 valuations 也会整句错位。

**逻辑关系**：因果——担忧的内容正是由其理由支撑的。

**参考译文**：令人担忧的是，建设 AI 数据中心和开发软件的成本最终可能高于企业能从中赚到的钱，因此一些科技公司也许无法证明其高得惊人的估值是合理的。

**写作迁移**：*The concern is that firms cannot justify the price because running costs will be higher than the revenue they generate from it.* 令人担忧的是，企业无法证明这个价格合理，因为运营成本会高于由此产生的收入。

### 对照让步与系表结构里的 up

**原句**：[28] While the Nasdaq closed 2.8% lower, it is up 7.8% over the last year.

**主干**：主句 `it is up 7.8% over the last year`，it 指纳斯达克指数；While 从句给出短期表现，与主句形成对照。

**层次与易错点**：`closed 2.8% lower` 里，2.8% lower 是说明 closed 结果的程度补语；`is up` 里的 up 是形容词作表语，不是动词。易错点：把 up 当作动词虽不妨碍大意，但会漏掉「当前处于上涨状态」这一层；把 While 理解为时间会丢掉短长期对照。

**逻辑关系**：对照——短期下跌与长期上涨并列呈现，提醒读者两者不可互相替代。

**参考译文**：尽管纳斯达克指数收跌 2.8%，但过去一年它仍上涨了 7.8%。

**写作迁移**：*While sales fell 5% last quarter, they are up 12% year on year.* 尽管上季度销售下降 5%，同比仍增长 12%。

## 术语与行业黑话

- **market value**｜本文含义：公司市值｜使用背景：由股价乘以流通股数估算，媒体常用「蒸发多少亿」描述单日变动｜近词辨析：market value 是通俗说法，估值语境里更常见 market capitalisation｜可否迁移到雅思：不可，写作中写 value of the company 即可。
- **valuation**｜本文含义：估值，即市场愿意给出的整体定价｜使用背景：多见于成长型公司评价，常与 justify、sky-high 搭配｜近词辨析：valuation 是判断，price 是成交价，value 是实际价值｜可否迁移到雅思：可（经济类大作文谈投资与定价时）。
- **referral traffic**｜本文含义：从搜索引擎或其他网站跳转来的访问量｜使用背景：内容平台的核心流量来源之一，与直接访问相对｜近词辨析：referral traffic 是来源之一，不等于全部用户数（daily users）｜可否迁移到雅思：不可，属行业内部说法。
- **earnings call**｜本文含义：上市公司发布季报后与分析师开的电话会｜使用背景：美股财报季的标准流程，管理层在会上回答提问，常出现 Looking ahead 这类前瞻表述｜近词辨析：earnings call 是会议，earnings report 是书面公告，earnings season 是整段财报季｜可否迁移到雅思：不可，写作直接写 quarterly results。
- **AI bubble**｜本文含义：市场估值可能高于未来业务所能支撑的水平｜使用背景：2023 年后随 AI 投资热潮反复出现，指一种担忧而非已确认事实｜近词辨析：bubble 是估值判断，crash 是已经发生的暴跌｜可否迁移到雅思：可（科技与投资类话题，需说明这是「担忧」）。
- **AI trade**｜本文含义：围绕 AI 概念股的整体押注与行情｜使用背景：交易员用语，把一类资产的操作合称 the X trade｜近词辨析：AI trade 指市场行为，AI industry 指产业本身｜可否迁移到雅思：不可。

## 背景补充

- **事实**：英特尔在 [11] 与 [12] 中同时给出两个方向的信息——电脑需求至少到明年上半年持续放缓，同时三季度设备投资已大幅增加、四季度还会更明显。这两句是理解它「为什么跌」的关键。
- **事实**：五家公司 2026 年合计承诺支出 8000 亿美元（[5]），亚马逊单独承诺 2200 亿（[14]），Alphabet 今年的投资上限是 2050 亿（[7]）。这些是计划与承诺，不等于已经发生的现金流出。
- **观点（Chris Low）**：他认为市场反应主要针对亚马逊、谷歌这类最大玩家，并把当前状况称为「成长阵痛」（[19][20]）。这是受访者判断，不是报道给出的结论。
- **观点（Kathleen Brookes）**：她认为投资者已变得强硬，对任何不及预期的信号都会惩罚，Alphabet 与英特尔正处在焦点位置（[25][26]）；她同时提到 11 月通常偏强（[29]），这是经验性说法，不是预测。

## 雅思迁移

**写作**

题目：*Some people believe companies should accept lower short-term profits in order to invest heavily in new technology. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*

提纲：
- 立场：部分同意——长期投入有必要，但必须能被回报路径证明。
- 主体一：技术投入收益周期长，短期利润下滑是正常代价。
- 主体二：当投入无法说明回报来源时，它更接近风险而非投资，市场会据此重新定价。
- 结论：同意「可以接受短期利润下降」，前提是企业披露投入与预期回报的关系。

示范段（78 词）：

> Large-scale investment in new technology rarely pays off immediately. Firms that build data centres or retrain staff may report lower profits for several quarters, and this is a reasonable price for long-term productivity. However, patience should not be unconditional. When companies cannot explain how the spending will generate returns, investors are right to treat it as risk rather than investment. Accepting lower short-term profits therefore makes sense only when the link between cost and future benefit is visible.

**阅读**

1. 报道为什么把 Alphabet 与英特尔的下跌分开叙述？
   定位：[6]–[12]
   依据："the AI summaries it now provides ... were affecting its core search advertising revenue"
   答案：两者原因不同。Alphabet 是核心搜索广告收入受到自家 AI 摘要影响；英特尔是电脑需求走弱，同时仍计划加大 AI 支出。

2. 为什么单日跌幅不能说明这些公司一年来的表现？
   定位：[27]–[28]
   依据："it is up 7.8% over the last year"
   答案：报道同时给出单月与年度两个尺度。纳斯达克 10 月下跌，但过去一年仍上涨，短期波动不能代表长期趋势。

3. 报道用什么说明投资者态度的变化？
   定位：[24]–[26]
   依据："Investors have turned ruthless, punishing any signs of disappointment"
   答案：引用 XTB 研究主管 Kathleen Brookes 的判断，说投资者对任何不及预期的迹象都会惩罚，Alphabet 与英特尔成为焦点。

4. 文中的「AI 泡沫」指什么？
   定位：[22]–[23]
   依据："will not be able to justify their sky-high valuations"
   答案：指一种担忧——部分公司的估值高于其未来业务可能支撑的水平，因为建设与开发成本可能超过能赚回的钱。

5. 报道对泡沫是否已破裂给出结论了吗？
   定位：[27]、[30]
   依据："it could suggest the AI trade is past its peak"
   答案：没有下定论。用词是 could suggest，属于对可能性的描述；报道还指出多数公司股价仍高于一年前。

## 口语延伸

**Part 2**

> **Describe a business news story that you found interesting.**
> You should say:
> - what the story was about
> - where you read it
> - what surprised you
> and explain why it matters to you.

2 分钟提纲（52 词）：`I read a BBC piece about tech shares falling as AI spending nears a trillion dollars. What surprised me was that even firms promising to spend more were punished. It shows markets now judge spending by returns, not ambition. It matters because AI costs reach ordinary people through prices and jobs.`

**Part 3**

1. Why do investors react so strongly to company spending plans?（出处：[4]）
   思路：先讲预期管理，再讲现金与回报的时间差。
   *Investors price in **future returns**, so a plan with no visible payoff reads as risk rather than ambition. When money gets more expensive to borrow, **holding cash** also looks safer than expanding.*

2. Should governments limit how much companies invest in AI?（出处：[23]）
   思路：区分投资规模与投资风险，不把话题引向报道没有的监管结论。
   *I doubt size alone needs a limit. What matters is whether **disclosure** lets the public see the risks, especially when the same firms control both the models and the data. Without that, regulation is guesswork.*

3. How might ordinary people be affected by this investment cycle?（出处：[11][18]）
   思路：从就业、订阅价格、服务可得性三点作答。
   *If the spending slows, **job cuts** in hardware firms usually come first. Consumers may also find AI features bundled into subscriptions they already pay for, which raises the price of things they never asked to upgrade.*
